About Me

Amy Lauren

Dancer · Zumba Instructor · Model

Amy Lauren is a certified Zumba instructor, dancer, and model known for her infectious energy and genuinely welcoming spirit. Under her motto "SLAY ALL DAY WITH AMY", she has built a thriving dance-fitness community where students of all levels feel empowered, confident, and at home on the dance floor.

Her classes are more than just a workout — they're a celebration of movement, joy, and self-expression. Whether you're stepping on the dance floor for the first time or looking to elevate your fitness journey, Amy's classes are the perfect blend of fun, movement, and confidence.
